Help

How to play, and what to do if something isn't working.

How to play

  • Squad: 2 to 6 players, each on their own iPhone or iPad, on the same Wi-Fi. One taps Host a game, the others join from Games nearby. Everyone stands by the host and points where they point to sync up, hides theirs, then hunts everyone else's.
  • Swarm: one device, handed round. Drop up to ten that blend in by themselves, hand over, and the seeker gets a countdown.
  • Paint & Hide: one chameleon you paint to match its spot, on a device with LiDAR.
  • From further away they're harder to see. Every find earns coins, more the quicker you are; spend them on skins. The Hint key is there all hunt, and after using it each find earns half the coins. Give up shows where they were.

Questions

Paint & Hide says it needs LiDAR

Painting a chameleon to match its spot uses a 3D scan, which needs the LiDAR scanner on iPhone Pro (12 Pro and later) and iPad Pro (2020 and later). Swarm and Squad work on any device that runs the game.

I can't see my friend's game

Be on the same Wi-Fi, with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on. Allow Chameleon Hunt local network access: Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. A game that has already started can't take new players — join the next round.

Sync up takes a long time

Everyone only has to sync with the host. Stand next to them and point at what they're pointing at, somewhere with plenty of detail — a bookshelf or a rug, not a bare wall or plain floor. Move slowly.

The chameleon won't go where I point

Sweep the camera slowly over the floor or a table until it lands. Without LiDAR, chameleons go on flat surfaces the camera has found.

Someone dropped out of a Squad game

Their place is kept for 30 seconds while their phone reconnects by itself. Keep the app open — locking the phone or switching apps drops the connection.

No sound

Sound follows the ring/silent switch, and there's a Sound effects switch in the game's Settings.

Still stuck?

Email help.wheelgrip@gmail.com with your device model and what happened. We read everything.
