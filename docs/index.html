Chameleon Hunt

Hide and seek, in your actual room. Put a chameleon anywhere, make it vanish, and hand over the phone. They get a countdown to find it.

Coming to the App Store

Three ways to play

Squad

2 to 6 players, each on their own device. Hide yours, hunt everyone else's.

Swarm

One device, passed round. Drop up to ten around the room. Each one blends in by itself.

Paint & Hide

One chameleon, painted by hand to match the spot. Needs LiDAR (iPhone Pro, iPad Pro).

Real things hide it

Tuck it behind the sofa and the sofa really covers it. It changes colour while it waits — stand still and it melts into the room; move fast and it can't keep up.

No accounts

No sign-up, no ads, nothing collected. Squad games talk only to the devices in the same room.
