Privacy

Chameleon Hunt collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no server.

The camera

The game uses the camera to show your room and to hide chameleons in it. Everything the camera sees — including the 3D scan of a hiding spot, on devices with LiDAR — is processed on your device and never leaves it, except as described under Squad games below. Nothing is stored after you leave a round.

Squad games

In a Squad game, devices in the same room connect to each other directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth (Apple's MultipeerConnectivity), which is why the game asks for local network access. They exchange only what the game needs: player names, game moves, the chameleons' positions and colours, and the AR map data that lets every device see them in the same place. This goes only to the other devices in that game, encrypted, and none of it goes to us or anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your settings — the name you play under, the hunt length, whether sound is on — are saved on your device so they're there next time. Delete the app and they're gone.

Sharing

If you tap Share after a round, the game makes a picture of the room and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you; the game keeps no copy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does. Questions: see Help.
