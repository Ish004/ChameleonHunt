## Description (4000 max)

```
Hide and seek, in your actual room.

Put a chameleon anywhere — on the bookshelf, under the table, behind the plant — and make it disappear. Paint it to match what's around it, or let it blend in by itself. Then hand the phone to a friend. They get a countdown to find it and tap it through the camera.

Real things hide it. Tuck it behind the sofa and the sofa really does cover it, so the best hiding spots are the ones you'd use yourself.

THREE WAYS TO PLAY

• Squad — everyone on their own iPhone or iPad, 2 to 6 players on the same Wi-Fi. Hide yours, hunt everyone else's. Every find turns the finder's colour, with their name on it, and the scoreboard sorts it out.
• Swarm — one phone, passed round. Drop up to ten around the room in a minute; each one camouflages itself. Find as many as you can before time runs out.
• Paint & Hide — one chameleon, painted by hand in a 3D studio with colours scanned from the spot. Needs a device with LiDAR (iPhone Pro or iPad Pro).

The further away you are, the harder they are to see, so you'll have to search the room. Stuck? Use the hint, or give up and see where they were.

COINS AND SKINS

Every find pays coins, more the quicker you are. Spend them on skins for your chameleon: colours, stripes, spots, gold. The hint costs half a hunt's coins, so use it wisely. Nothing to buy with money, ever.

THE CHAMELEON

It changes colour while it waits. Stand still and it melts into what's behind it; move fast and it can't quite keep up — which is how sharp-eyed seekers catch it.

SHARE THE PUZZLE

Finished a round? Send a picture of the room with the chameleon still hidden, and see if anyone can spot it. Then send the answer.

NO ACCOUNTS, NOTHING COLLECTED

No sign-up, no ads, no data collected. Squad games talk only to the devices in the same room.

Paint & Hide needs LiDAR (iPhone 12 Pro and later Pro models, iPad Pro 2020 and later). Swarm and Squad work on any iPhone or iPad that runs the app.
```

## Review notes (App Review Information → Notes)

```
ChameleonHunt is augmented-reality hide and seek played in a real room.

To try it on one device: tap Swarm, drop a few chameleons on the floor or a table (they appear on flat surfaces the camera has found), tap Done hiding, then Go. Tap the chameleons you can see.

Paint & Hide needs a LiDAR device (iPhone Pro / iPad Pro); on other devices it is shown locked with an explanation, and Swarm plays in a lite mode.

Squad needs two or more devices on the same Wi-Fi: one taps Squad → Host a game, the other joins it from Games nearby. The app asks for local network access only for this. A video of a full Squad game: <paste an unlisted YouTube link>

Practice painting (brush icon, top left of the home screen) opens the paint studio with a built-in room and works anywhere.

No account or sign-in is needed. The app collects no data.
```
